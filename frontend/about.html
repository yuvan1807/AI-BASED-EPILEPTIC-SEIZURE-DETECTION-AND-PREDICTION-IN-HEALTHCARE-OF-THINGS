About SeizureGuard AI

Revolutionizing epilepsy care through artificial intelligence

Who We Are

SeizureGuard AI is a pioneering healthcare technology company dedicated to transforming epilepsy management through cutting-edge artificial intelligence. Founded by a team of neurologists, data scientists, and engineers, we're committed to providing real-time seizure detection solutions that save lives.

Our deep learning models are trained on thousands of EEG samples from the CHB-MIT database, achieving 98% accuracy in detecting epileptic seizures. We integrate seamlessly with Internet of Healthcare Things (IoHT) devices, enabling continuous monitoring and instant alerts.

With a mission to make epilepsy care accessible, accurate, and affordable, we're working with hospitals, research institutions, and patients worldwide to bring peace of mind to millions living with epilepsy.

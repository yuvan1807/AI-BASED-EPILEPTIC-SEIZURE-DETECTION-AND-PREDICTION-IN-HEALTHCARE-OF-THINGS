Frequently Asked Questions

What EEG formats are supported?

We support PNG, JPG, JPEG, and BMP formats for EEG signal images. The image should clearly show the EEG waveform pattern.

How accurate is the detection?

Our model achieves 98% accuracy in detecting epileptic seizures, validated on thousands of EEG samples from CHB-MIT database.

How long does analysis take?

The entire process takes less than 10 seconds from upload to results, with most analyses completing in 5 seconds.

Is my data secure?

Yes, all uploaded images are processed securely and automatically deleted after analysis. We never store patient data.
